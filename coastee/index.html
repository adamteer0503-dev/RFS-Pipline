FAQ

Good questions.

Does it work on a normal table?

Yes. Fold the legs underneath and CoasTee sits flat like a regular coaster on tables, decks and countertops.

What ground does it work on?

The golf-tee legs are designed for grass, sand, dirt and other soft, uneven ground: lawns, beaches, parks, fairways and campsites.

Can I put my own photo or logo on it?

Yes. Use the design studio to upload a photo, logo or monogram, add text around the rim, and see it in real-life scenes before you order.
